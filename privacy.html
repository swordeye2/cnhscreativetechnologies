🛡️ Student Data Privacy Statement

The RP2040 MicroPython Laboratory Suite (rpithon.com) is built specifically for educational environments. We respect student privacy and do not require user accounts, email registration, passwords, or personal identity collection to access any simulator or review module.

👶 COPPA & Student Safety Compliance

In accordance with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), rpithon.com does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

  • No Personal Tracking: We do not ask for or store real names, home addresses, or contact information.
  • Non-Personalized Ads: Google AdSense is explicitly configured to serve non-personalized ads only across the entire domain. Advertisements are selected based on contextual content rather than student behavioral tracking.
// AdSense Non-Personalized Mode Configuration
(adsbygoogle = window.adsbygoogle || []).requestNonPersonalizedAds = 1;

🍪 Cookies & Third-Party Vendors

While rpithon.com itself does not place tracking cookies, third-party vendors (including Google AdSense) may use cookies to serve non-personalized ads or manage frequency capping and ad performance reporting.

  • Users can manage or disable cookie preferences through their browser settings at any time.
  • For more information on how Google uses data in advertising, visit Google's Advertising Policies.
