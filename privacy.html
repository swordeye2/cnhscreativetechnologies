The RP2040 MicroPython Laboratory Suite (rpithon.com), founded and authored by Jorge Victor M. Sales, Ph.D., is committed to providing a transparent, secure, and privacy-first digital learning environment for secondary, tertiary, and independent STEM learners.

Because our visualizers and interactive mechatronic models are designed primarily for classroom and self-guided educational use, our core guiding principle is simple: We do not collect, harvest, store, or sell personal identifiable information (PII) from any user.

🛡️ Children's Online Privacy Protection (COPPA) & Student Safety

In compliance with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and international educational safety protocols, rpithon.com does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 years of age.

  • No Account Registration Required: Access to all interactive HTML5 simulation modules, circuit schematics, and MicroPython code visualizers is completely anonymous and open. No account creation, login credentials, or email disclosures are required.
  • Client-Side Local Storage: User preferences (such as Catppuccin light/dark theme toggles and session-based quiz scores) are saved exclusively inside your local web browser (`localStorage` / `sessionStorage`) and are never transmitted to external servers.

📊 Technical Server Logs & Analytics

Like standard web servers, hosting platforms (such as GitHub Pages and CDN edge nodes) may log routine technical request header data, including:

  • IP addresses (processed anonymously for geographical request routing)
  • Browser type, language settings, and operating system version
  • Referring site URLs and page timestamp access logs

This diagnostic information is utilized strictly to monitor system performance, optimize PWA service worker caching, and safeguard web application infrastructure against malicious traffic.
