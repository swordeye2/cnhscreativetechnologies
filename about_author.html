📖 Biography & Academic Overview

Jorge Victor M. Sales, Ph.D. is an educator and researcher serving under the Department of Education (DepEd) – Schools Division of Catanduanes, and is affiliated with De La Salle University–Manila as a graduate researcher and former Research Assistant. He earned his Doctor of Philosophy in Science Education (Major in Physics) from De La Salle University–Manila, where his doctoral dissertation focused on Just-in-Time Teaching (JiTT-M) of Mechanics, integrating PhET simulations, the Feynman problem-solving approach, and metacognition through reflection.

He holds a Master of Arts in Teaching Physics from Catanduanes State University, with thesis research on teacher efficacy and performance in secondary science, and graduated Cum Laude with a Bachelor of Secondary Education.

🌐 Community Extension & ICT Engagement

In addition to his academic research in physics pedagogy, technology-enhanced learning, and action research learning communities, Dr. Sales actively engages in community extension and capacity building.

  • Resource Person for DICT Region V (Jan 2023 – Jun 2025): Delivered technical guidance, lectures, and workshop planning for DICT Catanduanes.
  • Digital Skills & ICT Literacy: Led workshops aimed at enhancing technology integration among secondary school educators and local community stakeholders.
  • Robotics & STEM Initiatives: Designing open-access mechatronics, micro-controller, and simulation frameworks for public schools.
🔬 Research Focus & Recognition
  • Physics Pedagogy & Metacognition: Developing JiTT and interactive simulations to improve problem-solving mastery in mechanics and mechatronics.
  • Data-Driven Instructional Innovations: Action research and meta-analyses on blended learning strategies in high school physics.
  • Graduate Research Mentorship: Guiding educators and researchers in data analysis, pedagogical design, and academic publishing.
🏆 Best Oral Presentation Award
13th International Conference on E-Education, E-Business, E-Management and E-Learning (IC4E 2022) for his meta-analysis on blended learning in high school physics.
