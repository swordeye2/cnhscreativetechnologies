🧲 Stepper Motor Coil Excitation Physics
Electromagnetic Torque & Step Sequence: Unipolar and bipolar stepper motors rotate by sequentially energizing internal stator coils (Coil A1, A2, B1, B2). In Full-Step Drive, two adjacent coils are energized simultaneously to maximize holding torque (angle step = 1.8° for 200-step motors). In Wave Drive, only one coil is active at a time for minimal power consumption.

Step Angle Calculation: Step Angle = 360° / N_steps (e.g., 360° / 200 steps = 1.8° per step). Standard Angle: 1.8° / Step 4-Pin Control Matrix
