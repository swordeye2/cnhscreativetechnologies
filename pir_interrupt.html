HC-SR501 PIR Motion Sensor Interrupt Simulator

Direct GPIO Digital Signals, Dual-Edge IRQ Triggers & Onboard LED Control

💡 Direct GPIO vs. I2C: The HC-SR501 PIR Sensor does not use complex serial bus data (I2C/SPI). Instead, it drives a single digital signal wire on GP16 HIGH (3.3V) when motion is detected and LOW (0V) when clear. MicroPython attaches an interrupt to catch both RISING and FALLING edges.
GP16 Sensor Output: LOGIC 0 (LOW / 0V)
Onboard LED (GP25): OFF
