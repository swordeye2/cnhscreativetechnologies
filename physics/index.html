Just in Time "GIT GUD" Physics

Interactive web-based and standalone offline simulation engine for optics, ballistics kinematics, harmonic motion, and field dynamics. Developed by Jorge Victor M. Sales (swordeye / thearbiter) under the CNHS Creative Technologies platform.

Optics
Single Thin Lens Engine
Biconvex, Plano-Convex, Biconcave, and Plano-Concave geometries with real-time ray tracing, focal extended virtual rays, and Lens Maker formula Inspector.
STATUS: ACTIVE Launch Module ➔
Optics
Compound Optics & Telescopes
Astronomical Refracting Telescope, Galilean Telescope, Compound Microscope, and Relay Lenses with multi-stage magnification calculation.
STATUS: ACTIVE Launch Module ➔
Kinematics
Projectile Motion & Trajectories
2D ballistic flight engine with real-time graph overlays (y-x, Vy-t, Vx-t), quadratic air resistance (k), and dynamic solution highlighting.
STATUS: ACTIVE Launch Module ➔
Harmonics
Simple Harmonic Motion & Pendulums
Simple, physical, and damped pendulum oscillators with phase-space diagrams and live energy conservation tracking (PE vs KE).
STATUS: UPCOMING View Roadmap ➔
Waves
Ripple Tank & Wave Interference
2D wave tank simulating Young's double-slit interference, wave superposition, diffraction, and standing wave nodes.
STATUS: UPCOMING View Roadmap ➔
Circuits
DC Circuit Dynamics & Kirchhoff's Laws
Interactive schematic builder with resistor networks, voltage dividers, dynamic current flow vectors, and matrix mesh analysis.
