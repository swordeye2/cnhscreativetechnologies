📖 Professional Biography

Jorge Victor M. Sales, Ph.D. is an educator and researcher serving under the Department of Education (DepEd) – Schools Division of Catanduanes, and is affiliated with De La Salle University–Manila as a graduate researcher and former Research Assistant.

He earned his Doctor of Philosophy in Science Education (Major in Physics) from De La Salle University–Manila, where his doctoral dissertation focused on Just-in-Time Teaching (JiTT-M) of Mechanics, integrating PhET simulations, the Feynman problem-solving approach, and metacognition through reflection. He holds a Master of Arts in Teaching Physics from Catanduanes State University, with thesis research on teacher efficacy and performance in secondary science, and graduated Cum Laude with a Bachelor of Secondary Education.

In addition to his academic research in physics pedagogy, technology-enhanced learning, and action research learning communities, Dr. Sales actively engages in community extension and capacity building. He served as a Resource Person for the Department of Information and Communications Technology (DICT) – Region V Catanduanes from January 2023 to June 2025, delivering lectures, technical guidance, and workshop planning to enhance digital skills, ICT literacy, and technology integration among educators and community stakeholders.

Dr. Sales was recognized with the Best Oral Presentation Award at the 13th International Conference on E-Education, E-Business, E-Management and E-Learning (IC4E 2022) for his meta-analysis on blended learning in high school physics. He continues to contribute to data-driven instructional innovations, robotics initiatives, and graduate-level research mentorship.
