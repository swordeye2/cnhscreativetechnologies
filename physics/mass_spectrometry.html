💡 Spectrometry Physics Principles
1. Velocity Selector (E/B₁): Passes ions with constant speed $v = E/B_1$.
2. Magnetic Sector (B₂): Bends ions into circular arc $r = \frac{m \cdot v}{q \cdot B_2}$. Lighter isotopes curve tighter!
3. Packing Fraction ($f$): Aston defined $f = \frac{M_{\text{actual}} - A}{A} \times 10^4$. Negative values reveal missing mass converted into binding energy ($E_b = \Delta m \cdot c^2$).
