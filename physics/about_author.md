## 💡 Key Contributions & Research

### 1. The MeJITPHET Pedagogical Framework
Dr. Sales developed and implemented **MeJITPHET**, an instructional methodology designed for high school physics and mechatronics. The framework operationalizes a dual-loop system between learners and teachers by uniting:
* **Just-in-Time Teaching (JiTT):** Pre-activity diagnostic prompts that adjust real-time instruction around student misconceptions.
* **Feynman-Style Pedagogy:** Intuitive, stepped visual hooks that uncover core physical laws.
* **Interactive Web Simulations & Hardware Visualizers:** Interactive tools mapping MicroPython register logic directly to real-world physical dynamics (e.g., PWM generation, motor torque, sensor interrupts, closed-loop PID control).

### 2. Community Extension & Capacity Building
* **Resource Person (Jan 2023 – Jun 2025):** Department of Information and Communications Technology (**DICT – Region V Catanduanes**). Delivered technical guidance, workshop planning, and lectures to elevate digital literacy, ICT integration, and tech capabilities among educators and community stakeholders.
