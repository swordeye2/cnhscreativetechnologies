Nuclear Physics Summary

6 Quark Flavors: Up ($u$), Down ($d$), Charm ($c$), Strange ($s$), Top ($t$), Bottom ($b$)[cite: 1].

Color Confinement: Single quarks carry color charge and can NEVER exist freely[cite: 1]. They bind into color-neutral Hadrons ($qqq$ or $q\bar{q}$)[cite: 1].

Force Carriers: Gluons ($g$) mediate the strong nuclear interaction between color-charged quarks[cite: 1].
