Subatomic Building Blocks vs. Force Carriers:
• Fermions (The Matter): Quarks, Leptons, and Baryons form physical matter[cite: 1]. They obey Pauli Exclusion so matter occupies physical space[cite: 1].
• Bosons (The Glue & Forces): Gauge bosons (Gluons, Photons, W/Z) act as "force glue" exchanged between fermions to attract, repel, bind, or transform them[cite: 1].
• Mesons as Residual Nuclear Glue: Composite integer-spin bosons ($q\bar{q}$) mediate the residual strong force binding protons and neutrons inside atomic nuclei[cite: 1].
Fundamental Interactions & Spin Rules:
• Strong Force (Quark Glue): Mediated by Gluons ($g$). Binds quarks into hadrons via color charge confinement[cite: 1].
• Electromagnetic Force (Atomic Glue): Mediated by Photons ($\gamma$). Binds electrons to nuclei and powers chemical bonds[cite: 1].
• Weak Force (Flavor Transformations): Mediated by $W^\pm$ and $Z^0$ bosons[cite: 1]. Drives radioactive beta decay and stellar fusion.
• Spin Addition Rule: Odd quark counts ($qqq$) yield half-integer net spin (Fermions); even counts ($q\bar{q}$) yield integer net spin (Bosons)[cite: 1].
