Set Theory Relation:

Baryons ⊂ Hadrons
Mesons ⊂ Hadrons

∴ "Every Baryon is a Hadron, but NOT every Hadron is a Baryon!"
  • Baryons (qqq): Composed of 3 valence quarks (Half-integer spin = Composite Fermions)[cite: 1].
  • Mesons (q-q̄): Composed of 1 quark + 1 antiquark (Integer spin = Composite Bosons)[cite: 1].
