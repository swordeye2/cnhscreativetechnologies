Set Theory Rule:

Baryons ⊂ Hadrons
Mesons ⊂ Hadrons

∴ "Every Baryon is a Hadron, but NOT every Hadron is a Baryon!"

• Baryons ($qqq$): Made of 3 quarks $\rightarrow$ Half-Integer Spin $\rightarrow$ Composite Fermions[cite: 1].
• Mesons ($q\bar{q}$): Made of 1 quark + 1 antiquark $\rightarrow$ Integer Spin $\rightarrow$ Composite Bosons[cite: 1].
