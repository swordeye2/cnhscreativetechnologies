📐 Key Taxonomy Set Rules
• Fermions (Spin-1/2): Quarks & Leptons ($e^-, \mu^-, \tau^-, \nu_e, \nu_\mu, \nu_\tau$). Obey Pauli Exclusion.
• Muons ($\mu^-$) & Neutrinos ($\nu_\mu$): Gen-2 Leptons. $207\times$ heavier than electrons!
• Hadrons ($qqq$ & $q\bar{q}$): Composite systems held by gluons. Split into Baryons (Fermions) and Mesons (Bosons).
