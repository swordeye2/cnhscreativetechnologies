Quantum Geometric Models & Physical Representations:
• Quarks (Triangular / Hexagonal Nodes): Modeled as rotating geometric nodes holding localized color-field vectors ($R, G, B$).
• Leptons (Spherical Point-Cloud Probabilities): Modeled as point-like probability clouds surrounded by spin vector orbits ($s = \pm 1/2$).
• Gauge Bosons (Undulating Wave Packets): Modeled as continuous transverse sine-wave packets ($\gamma, g, W, Z$) radiating force-field energy.
• Higgs Boson (Scalar Mass Field Ripple): Modeled as concentric scalar ripples conferring rest mass via electroweak symmetry breaking.
• Composite Hadrons (Multi-Nodular Valence Clusters): Modeled as orbiting valence quark clusters (Proton: $uud$, Neutron: $udd$) bound by glowing gluon exchange lines inside a color-confinement sphere.
Fundamental Interactions & Force Mediators:
• Strong Force (Gluon $g$): Binds quarks into hadrons. Massless, Spin-1, Color Charge.
• Electromagnetic Force (Photon $\gamma$): Interacts with charged particles. Massless, Spin-1.
• Weak Force ($W^+, W^-, Z^0$ Bosons): Responsible for flavor change & radioactive beta decay. Massive ($80.4 \text{ GeV}, 91.2 \text{ GeV}$).
• Higgs Field (Higgs Boson $H^0$): Scalar boson (Spin-0, $125.25 \text{ GeV}$) providing mass coupling.
