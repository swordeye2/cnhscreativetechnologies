📐 Essential Accelerator Formulas
1. Lorentz Factor:
γ = 1 / √(1 - v²/c²)
2. Relativistic Kinetic Energy:
K = (γ - 1) · m₀c²
3. Motion-to-Mass Defect:
Δm = K / c² (1 amu ≈ 0.9315 GeV)
4. Center-of-Mass Energy:
√s = 2 · γ · m₀c²
