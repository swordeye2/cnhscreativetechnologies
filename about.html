🌐 Educational Vision & STEM Purpose

The RP2040 MicroPython Laboratory Suite (hosted at rpithon.com) is an open-access, zero-installation STEM visualizer suite designed to support high school and undergraduate engineering education. It decouples complex physical phenomena—such as optical ray dynamics, motor torque vectors, gas diffusion hysteresis, and thermodynamic heat dissipation—from physical hardware costs and lab equipment constraints.

By combining interactive HTML5 Canvas simulators with real MicroPython code examples (register calls like PWM.duty_u16() and ADC.read_u16()), students build strong conceptual models before working on physical microcontrollers.

🎯 Core Learning Objectives

  • Interactive Physics Simulation: Explore wave refraction, thin-lens optics, rotational dynamics, and motor stall currents in real-time.
  • Hardware Control Abstraction: Learn how physical sensor inputs translate into 12-bit ADC registers and 16-bit PWM actuator outputs.
  • Universal Accessibility: Runs on any device with a modern web browser or mobile PWA without software installation.
  • Formative Self-Assessment: Integrated 40-minute diagnostic review exams allow learners to test their mastery of mechatronics concepts.
