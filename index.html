GPIO & Digital Logic
Direct 3.3V Input & Output LED Simulator
Visualize direct 3.3V digital signal inputs on assigned RP2040 GPIO pins (GP14, GP16, GP18) driving LED output circuits (GP15, GP17, GP19). Features pin-level visual glowing and live reactive MicroPython code inspection.

Electronics Physics
Internal Pull-Up vs Pull-Down Resistor Lab
Explore default idle voltages (0.0V LOW for Pull-Down vs 3.3V HIGH for Pull-Up) and active-LOW logic. Visualizes the internal ~50kΩ resistor bus inside the RP2040 chip during switch actuation.
Launch Pull-Resistor Lab →

Simulate RP2040 16-bit PWM hardware registers (`duty_u16`) and master bit-shifting arithmetic (`>>`) down to 8-bit, 10-bit, 12-bit, and 14-bit targets with an interactive oscilloscope waveform trace.
